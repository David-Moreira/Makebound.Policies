Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026
Last Updated: September 20, 2026

Privacy First: Makebound is designed with privacy in mind. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or otherwise receive personal data about its players.

1. Information Makebound Collects

Makebound does not collect personal information or usage data through the game.

In particular, Makebound does not collect or transmit:

  • your name, email address, or other contact information;
  • account or login information;
  • device identifiers;
  • IP addresses;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • gameplay analytics or usage statistics;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • browsing or tracking information;
  • payment information;
  • contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, or other device data; or
  • information for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Makebound does not currently require users to create an account.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Makebound may create and store information necessary for the game to function, such as:

  • game progress;
  • skill levels and progression;
  • completed or unlocked projects;
  • in-game resources;
  • achievements or similar gameplay state;
  • game preferences and settings; and
  • other locally generated save data.

This information is stored locally on your device.

We do not receive, access, or transmit this information to our servers.

You can generally remove locally stored Makebound data by deleting the game's saved data or uninstalling the game, subject to the functionality provided by your operating system or device.

Your device or operating-system provider may separately provide device backup or restoration functionality. Any processing performed by such a service is governed by the provider's own terms and privacy policy and is not controlled by Makebound.

3. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Makebound does not currently use third-party analytics services, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or profiling technologies.

The game does not use your activity to provide targeted advertising and does not sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Platforms and Stores

Makebound may be downloaded, purchased, installed, or updated through third-party platforms or application stores.

Those platform providers may independently collect information relating to your account, purchases, device, downloads, or use of their services.

Such collection is performed by the relevant platform provider and is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. Makebound does not control the platform provider's independent collection or processing of information.

5. Communications With Us

The Makebound game itself does not collect contact information.

If you voluntarily contact us outside the game, for example by sending an email requesting support, we may receive the information that you choose to include in that communication, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

We will use that information only as reasonably necessary to respond to your communication, resolve the issue you contacted us about, comply with applicable law, or protect our legal rights.

We will not use support communications for advertising or sell the information contained in them.

6. Children's Privacy

Makebound does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the game.

Because the game does not collect or transmit personal information from its users, Makebound does not use children's personal information for advertising, profiling, or other commercial purposes.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us through a direct communication outside the game, you may contact us using the details below to request its deletion.

7. Data Sharing and Sale

Makebound does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data collected through the game because the game does not collect such data.

Makebound does not provide player information to advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or marketing companies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Makebound does not maintain a server-side database of player accounts, gameplay histories, or other player personal information.

Gameplay and save information stored locally on your device remains there until it is deleted through the game, the device or operating system, or by uninstalling the game, as applicable.

Information voluntarily provided when contacting us may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the communication, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect our legal rights.

You may contact us to request deletion of personal information that you previously provided directly to us.

9. International Data Transfers

Because Makebound does not transmit player data from the game to us, we do not transfer gameplay or player data internationally through Makebound.

If you contact us directly, your communication may be processed in the country where we operate or where our communications service providers operate, subject to applicable data-protection requirements.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Because Makebound does not collect or maintain personal information about you through the game, we ordinarily have no player account or gameplay information that we can access, provide, correct, export, or delete.

If you have directly provided personal information to us, such as through a support request, you may contact us regarding that information and any privacy rights available to you under applicable law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Makebound may change over time.

If a future version of Makebound introduces features that collect or transmit personal information—for example, cloud saves, user accounts, multiplayer functionality, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, online leaderboards, or other online services—this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices.

Where required by law, appropriate notice or consent will be provided before newly introduced personal information processing takes place.

The date at the top of this Privacy Policy indicates when it was most recently updated.
